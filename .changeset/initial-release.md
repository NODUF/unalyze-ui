First release for the consuming team to try.

24 components: `Surface`, `Text`, `Heading`, `Button`, `IconButton`, `Input`, `Textarea`, `Label`,
`FormField`, `Checkbox`, `Switch`, `Badge`, `Divider`, `Spinner`, `Skeleton`, `EmptyState`,
`Dialog`, `Popover`, `Tooltip`, `Select`, `DropdownMenu`, `Tabs`.

Dark theme only, single-face Nohemi, precompiled CSS — the consuming application needs no Tailwind
and no build changes. Overlay behaviour comes from Radix; the visual language is read off the Figma
dashboard.

Components with no design behind them yet are marked `@derived` in their source. Their behaviour
and accessibility are settled; their appearance may change in a later minor.
